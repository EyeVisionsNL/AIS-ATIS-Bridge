# Audio and recordings
## Listen live
Start the receiver with **Save and start**, then click **Audio: off**. Audio is off whenever the page loads and needs a click to begin.

Adjust **Volume** on the dashboard; 0% mutes playback. Sound plays through the browser device’s speakers or headphones, using live mono 16 kHz audio from the same receiver feed used for ATIS decoding.

## Replay the last transmission
**Recent Marine transmissions** keeps up to four squelch-gated recordings. Each can be up to two minutes long, with WAV replay available after the signal ends.

These recordings live in memory. They are not a permanent archive and disappear when the Bridge process restarts.

## Listen less, keep receiving
Turning live audio off does not stop decoding, scanning or recording. Use **Stop receiver** when you want to stop the voice receiver itself.

## No sound?
1. Confirm the receiver is running and the selected channel has a signal.
2. Click the audio button; page loads do not start listening automatically.
3. Check dashboard volume and your browser device’s output volume.
4. If the browser suspended a background tab, click Audio again.
5. Review squelch and gain in [[Receiver and channels]].

No recording during a quiet period is normal: capture depends on squelch opening for a transmission.
